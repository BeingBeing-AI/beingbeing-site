01 / Our product

Available now

Twoo — an AI that
belongs to both of you

Twoo gives every pair a space of its own: you, someone you choose, and a small octopus with a big personality. Each pair gets its own island with a shared chat, and an AI companion that follows the conversation you both have — not just one side of it.

01 /

Talk as three

One island per pair, with a shared chat. Your octopus jumps in with ideas, jokes, and quick answers, reading the conversation you share.

02 /

Plan together

Weekend plans, trips, where to eat. Ask your octopus right in the chat, and set shared reminders for birthdays and anniversaries.

03 /

Keep what matters

The octopus notebook keeps the places, plans, and promises you mention, so you can find them again later.

A space for the two of you.
